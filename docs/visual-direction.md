# CarleCool – Visual Direction (Unified Creative Director, 27 Nov 2026)

**Goal:** aristocratic melancholy meets raw 1990s street culture and authentic analog music
production. Every film feels like a lost fragment of a cinematic world: nostalgic, rebellious,
intimate, imperfect and unmistakably CarleCool.

Two worlds, one musical identity. The binding details of each world (themes, motif weights,
search words, image prompts, look, grade, effects, text lines) live in the director files:

- `directors/lana.json` – LANA, The Narrative Collision: Kennedy & Onassis, Royals & Highlands,
  90s minimalism, street skate, analog music.
- `directors/night.json` – NIGHT, Concrete Crown & Midnight Horsepower: NYC projects, baseball
  under floodlights, Porsche 993 night drive, nocturnal skate, old money at night, underground
  analog studio.

## Binding rules (code, every director)

- One theme (film idea) per Reel; every scene serves it.
- Analog music production (MPC, synthesizers, drum machines, Rhodes/piano, mixing desks, tape,
  vinyl, boombox) in every Reel when licensed footage is available (signature role `gear`).
- No AI clips, no nudity, women only as people on screen, no romantic couples, no brand logos,
  no generic influencer looks, no nature/landscape filler, the owner's clip blocklist.
- Celebrities (Kennedys, Onassis, Lady Di, Kate Moss, Muska, Mullen) are aesthetic references
  only – never their likeness.
- Zoom only on footage that stands still; no effect for its own sake.
- Black and white is a whole-Reel decision, never mixed with colour.
- Fewer, better scenes over many mediocre ones. No new running costs without approval.
