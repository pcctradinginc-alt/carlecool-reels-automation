# Repository audit – 27 Nov 2026 (Phase 0)

Scope: all 50 modules of `carlecool/` (11 705 lines), the 17 workflows, `data/`, 52 test
files and the production / performance data. Branch `dev/cinematic-system`.
Each finding gives the evidence, the impact on video quality, and the planned phase.

## B. Random or hash-based creative choices (20 places)

`sha256(run_key|…)` decides:
- the theme tie-break
- the start of the theme words
- the car brand
- the hook line
- the hook strategy
- the start of the archive search
- the archive segment
- the Wikimedia pick and its zoom direction
- the order of the signature queries
- the B/W Reel decision
- exposure jitter
- the storyboard rotation (`cinematic.build_storyboard`)
- the caption, its tags and its question

Some of these are intentional A/B randomisation and are correct: text hook, hook strategy, B/W share. The rest are **creative choices without a reason**: car brand, theme words, archive segment, storyboard rotation. → Phase 1/3: the Creative Plan decides these from the film idea and the material. Hashes remain only as a documented tie-break and for real A/B assignment.

## F. Quality checks

- `quality_gate.inspect_reel` is technical: size, duration, black and frozen frames, audio level. Good.
- The Visual Critic (D) measures the global picture. It does **not** check the first two seconds separately, motif clarity, stock-video character or compression artefacts.
- Thresholds are set by hand and not calibrated (Phase 4/5).
- True peak and loudness are normalised (`loudnorm I=-14 TP=-1`) but not checked after the AAC encode (Phase 4).

## Baseline (Phase 0)

`scripts/compare_pipelines.py` renders the old pipeline (`6c75a7c`, before directors / A–D) and the current one under identical conditions:
- same song excerpt and same length,
- same frozen footage pool (`scripts/baseline_inputs.py`: archive clips with a fixed key + library files),
- the same neutral measurements: critic, coherence on the same Shot DNA, loudness / true peak, size, render time, memory.

It produces a blind A/B pair (`scripts/mail_comparison.py`; the key is sealed in the log). The workflow `baseline-compare.yml` runs it manually.

**Local smoke test** on synthetic test pictures (no statement about image quality, only that the harness works):

| measure | old | new |
|---|---|---|
| duration | 16.0 | 16.0 |
| render time s | 37.7 | 29.9 |
| coherence (neutral) | 0.704 | 0.717 |
| critic score | 0.982 | 0.987 |

A real baseline needs a real song and real footage. It runs as soon as the owner approves the run.
